How the ranking works

implied = TVL × comp × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Comp — the market's own price for the category: the median mcap/TVL of the live-token protocols in it (at least three), else the global median. Clamped to 0.05–1.00.
  • Growth — 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60%, halved. A protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking at the same TVL.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + up to 0.30 for up to two years listed. A protocol that has run for two years without a token has more to price in than one listed last month.
  • Breadth — +2% per extra chain, up to nine. Multi-chain reach is real distribution.

Pre-token means the snapshot records no token for the protocol — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is not proof it has no token: the record may be incomplete. Implied value is a model's estimate of what a launch would be worth, not a price, and the farm plans are starting points, not advice. The board never ranks a wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned product, and no protocol name is ever special-cased — the same function scores every row.
